Nutrition & Health

NutritionFacts.org
Evidence-based nutrition information from Dr. Michael Greger. Videos and articles on plant-based health.
Cronometer
Track your nutrition to ensure you're meeting all vitamin and mineral requirements.
The Vegan RD
Evidence-based vegan nutrition guidance from registered dietitian Ginny Messina.
Vegan Health
Comprehensive nutrition recommendations from dietitian Jack Norris, co-founder of Vegan Outreach.

New to Veganism

Challenge 22
Free 22-day vegan challenge with mentors, recipes, and community support.
Vegan Kit
Simple guide covering why and how to go vegan, with practical tips.
The Vegan Society - How to Go Vegan
Step-by-step transition guide from the organisation that coined the term "vegan."

Activism & Outreach

Anonymous for the Voiceless
Organised street activism using "Cubes of Truth" plus one-on-one conversations.
Direct Action Everywhere (DxE)
Grassroots animal rights network with chapters worldwide. Resources for organising direct action.
Vegan Outreach
Creating a vegan world through literature distribution and one-on-one conversations.
The Humane League
Corporate campaigns and grassroots organising to end abuse of animals raised for food.

Talking to Non-Vegans

Carnism Awareness & Action Network
Understanding carnism (the invisible belief system that conditions people to eat animals) to have better conversations.
Your Vegan Fallacy Is
Concise, evidence-based responses to common arguments against veganism.
Earthling Ed
YouTube channel featuring respectful, effective vegan advocacy and street interviews.

Animal Sanctuaries

Farm Sanctuary
Rescue, advocacy, and education. Sanctuaries in California and New York.
Woodstock Farm Sanctuary
Animal rescue and vegan advocacy in New York's Hudson Valley.
Global Federation of Animal Sanctuaries
Find accredited animal sanctuaries worldwide.

Environmental Impact

Our World in Data - Environmental Impacts of Food
Data-driven analysis of food production's environmental footprint.
Cowspiracy Facts
Sourced facts about animal agriculture's environmental impact from the documentary.
Avoiding Meat & Dairy - The Guardian
Report on the most comprehensive analysis to date of farming's environmental impact.
