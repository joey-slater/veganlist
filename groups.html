Ongoing vegan organisations, collectives, and community groups by city. These are established groups, not one-time events (for those, see meetups).

New York

NYC Vegan Drinks
Monthly social meetups at vegan-friendly bars across NYC boroughs.
meetup.com/nycvegandrinks
Collectively Free NYC
Intersectional animal rights activism connecting animal liberation with other social justice movements.
collectivelyfree.org

Los Angeles

Animal Acres
Farm sanctuary and education centre in Acton, CA offering tours and volunteer opportunities.
farmsanctuary.org/locations/la
Los Angeles Animal Save
Bearing witness to animals in transport, weekly vigils at slaughterhouses.
Facebook: LA Animal Save

San Francisco

Direct Action Everywhere (DxE) SF Bay
Grassroots animal rights network organising direct action campaigns and community events.
directactioneverywhere.com
VegOut SF
Social group organizing potlucks, restaurant outings, and vegan-friendly activities.
Facebook: VegOut SF

Portland

Portland Vegan Drinks
Monthly social gatherings at vegan restaurants and bars.
Instagram: @pdxvegandrinks
Pasado's Safe Haven
Animal sanctuary and advocacy organisation in Sultan, WA (near Portland area).
pasadosafehaven.org

Chicago

Chicago Vegan Family Network
Support network for vegan families with kids, organising playdates and family-friendly events.
Facebook: Chicago Vegan Family Network
Animal Advocacy Chicago
Activism and outreach focused on institutional change and corporate campaigns.
Email: info@animaladvocacychicago.org

London

London Vegan Meetup
One of the largest vegan social groups in the UK, organising regular restaurant outings and events.
meetup.com/londonvegan
Anonymous for the Voiceless London
Weekly Cube of Truth events across London neighbourhoods.
Instagram: @avlondon

Manchester

Manchester Vegan Society
Monthly socials, potlucks, and activism events across Greater Manchester.
Facebook: Manchester Vegan Society

Bristol
