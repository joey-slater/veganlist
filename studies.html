Peer-reviewed scientific research on plant-based diets, animal agriculture, and related topics. Open-access studies are marked; others may require institutional access or can be requested from authors.

Health & Nutrition

Position of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics: Vegetarian Diets OPEN ACCESS
Melina V, Craig W, Levin S. Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, 2016
Official position paper stating that appropriately planned vegetarian and vegan diets are healthful, nutritionally adequate, and may provide health benefits for the prevention and treatment of certain diseases.
Plant-Based Diets and Cardiovascular Health OPEN ACCESS
Hemler EC, Hu FB. Trends in Cardiovascular Medicine, 2019
Review finding plant-based diets are associated with lower risk of cardiovascular disease, cardiovascular disease mortality, and all-cause mortality.
Vegetarian Diets and the Risk of Diabetes OPEN ACCESS
Papier K, et al. Nutrition & Diabetes, 2017
Systematic review showing vegetarian diets are associated with lower risk of type 2 diabetes.
Vegetarian Dietary Patterns and Mortality ABSTRACT
Orlich MJ, et al. JAMA Internal Medicine, 2013
Adventist Health Study-2 cohort of 96,000 people finding vegetarian diets associated with lower mortality including reduced cardiovascular mortality.

Environmental Impact

Reducing Food's Environmental Impacts Through Producers and Consumers ABSTRACT
Poore J, Nemecek T. Science, 2018
Most comprehensive analysis to date of farming's environmental impacts. Found that avoiding meat and dairy is the single biggest way to reduce environmental impact on the planet.
Analysis and Valuation of Health and Climate Change Cobenefits of Dietary Change OPEN ACCESS
Springmann M, et al. PNAS, 2016
Global adoption of plant-based diets could reduce greenhouse gas emissions by up to 70% and save millions of lives annually.
Options for Keeping the Food System Within Environmental Limits ABSTRACT
Springmann M, et al. Nature, 2018
Analysis of pathways to sustainable food systems, finding dietary shifts toward plant-based foods essential to meet environmental targets.
Livestock and Climate Change OPEN ACCESS
Goodland R, Anhang J. World Watch Magazine, 2009
Analysis concluding livestock and their byproducts account for at least 51% of annual worldwide greenhouse gas emissions.

Animal Cognition & Sentience

Thinking Pigs: A Comparative Review of Cognition, Emotion, and Personality in Sus domesticus OPEN ACCESS
Marino L, Colvin CM. International Journal of Comparative Psychology, 2015
Comprehensive review of scientific literature on pig cognition, revealing complex cognitive abilities comparable to dogs and chimpanzees.
The Cambridge Declaration on Consciousness OPEN ACCESS
Low P, et al. Francis Crick Memorial Conference, 2012
International group of neuroscientists declaring that non-human animals possess neurological substrates of consciousness.
Fish Intelligence, Sentience and Ethics OPEN ACCESS
Brown C. Animal Sentience, 2015
Review of evidence for fish cognition, learning, memory, and sentience challenging traditional views of fish as simple reflex-driven animals.

Food Systems & Agriculture

Sustainability of Plant-Based Diets OPEN ACCESS
Fresán U, Sabaté J. Nutrients, 2019
Review of environmental sustainability comparing plant-based and animal-based diets across multiple metrics.
Assessing the Efficiency of Changes in Land Use for Mitigating Climate Change OPEN ACCESS
Hayek MN, et al. PNAS, 2020
Eliminating animal agriculture could reduce atmospheric CO2 for 30 years even without any other changes to emissions.
