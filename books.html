Community-curated reading list for vegans and the vegan-curious. From philosophy to cookbooks to fiction featuring vegan characters.

Philosophy & Ethics

Animal Liberation
Peter Singer
The foundational text of the modern animal rights movement. Philosophical argument against speciesism and for considering animal interests.
The Sexual Politics of Meat
Carol J. Adams
Feminist-vegetarian critical theory exploring connections between patriarchy and meat consumption.
Why We Love Dogs, Eat Pigs, and Wear Cows
Melanie Joy
Introduction to carnism - the invisible belief system that shapes our relationship with animals.
Eating Animals
Jonathan Safran Foer
Personal and investigative exploration of factory farming and the ethics of eating animals.
The Case for Animal Rights
Tom Regan
Rigorous philosophical argument for animal rights based on inherent value.

Non-Fiction & Memoir

The China Study
T. Colin Campbell & Thomas M. Campbell II
Nutritional research on the relationship between diet and disease, advocating for whole-food, plant-based eating.
How Not to Die
Michael Greger, MD
Evidence-based guide to preventing and reversing disease through nutrition.
The Lucky Ones: My Passionate Fight for Farm Animals
Jenny Brown
Memoir from the founder of Woodstock Farm Sanctuary about rescuing animals and fighting for their rights.
We Are the Weather
Jonathan Safran Foer
Exploration of climate change and the role of animal agriculture, arguing for collective dietary change.
Sistah Vegan
A. Breeze Harper (editor)
Anthology of Black female vegans on food, identity, health, and society.

Fiction

Tender is the Flesh
Agustina Bazterrica
Dystopian novel exploring speciesism and the commodification of sentient beings.
We, the Drowned
Carsten Jensen
Epic seafaring novel (not explicitly vegan but explores human relationship with the sea and its creatures).
Kafka on the Shore
Haruki Murakami
Surreal novel featuring a character who communicates with cats (Murakami is vegetarian).

Cookbooks

Veganomicon
Isa Chandra Moskowitz & Terry Hope Romero
Classic vegan cookbook with over 250 recipes covering basics to advanced techniques.
Thug Kitchen
Thug Kitchen LLC
Plant-based recipes with irreverent humor, focusing on whole foods and accessibility.
The Homemade Vegan Pantry
Miyoko Schinner
DIY staples like cheeses, butters, milks, and condiments from scratch.
Vegan Soul Kitchen
Bryant Terry
African American and Creole recipes reimagined as fresh, vegan comfort food.
Isa Does It
Isa Chandra Moskowitz
Quick vegan recipes that don't sacrifice flavor, organized by time commitment.

For Kids & Young Adults

That's Why We Don't Eat Animals
Ruby Roth
Picture book introducing young children to veganism and compassion for animals.
Vegan is Love
Ruby Roth
Children's book on living vegan beyond diet - clothing, entertainment, and everyday choices.
V is for Vegan
Ruby Roth
ABC book teaching the vegan alphabet to young children.
